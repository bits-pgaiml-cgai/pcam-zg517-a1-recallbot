# Imaging service notes

doc_id: SVC-IMAGING
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Services > Imaging
codes: IMG-XRAY, IMG-USG, IMG-MRI

Imaging service notes. IMG-XRAY is walk-in chest and limb X-ray at both locations. IMG-USG requires a booked slot; abdominal ultrasound needs 6 hours fasting. IMG-MRI is available only at LOC-BLR and needs AUTH-PRE for insured patients. Reports for IMG-XRAY are ready in 2 hours; IMG-USG in 4 hours; IMG-MRI in 24 hours. Pregnant patients must inform the technologist before any ionising study.  Additional operational guidance for Imaging service notes emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes IMG-XRAY, IMG-USG, IMG-MRI, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document SVC-IMAGING version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

# Clinic hours and locations FAQ

Clinic hours FAQ. Location code LOC-HYD is the Hyderabad Jubilee Hills centre. Location code LOC-BLR is the Bengaluru Koramangala centre. Weekday hours code HRS-WD: Monday to Saturday 08:00 to 20:00 for both centres. Sundays are closed except for LOC-HYD urgent care 09:00 to 13:00. Public holiday schedules are posted 7 days in advance on the portal. Parking is free for the first two hours at LOC-HYD and paid at LOC-BLR.

# Insurance and cashless FAQ

doc_id: FAQ-INSURANCE
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: FAQ > Insurance
codes: INS-STAR, INS-HDFC, AUTH-PRE

Insurance FAQ for ClinicCare. Cashless partners include INS-STAR (Star Health) and INS-HDFC (HDFC Ergo). Pre-authorisation code AUTH-PRE must be obtained for planned procedures above INR 15,000. GP consults under plan P-204 usually need no AUTH-PRE. Patients should carry the insurance card and a government photo ID. Denied cashless claims can be filed for reimbursement within 30 days.  Additional operational guidance for Insurance and cashless FAQ emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes INS-STAR, INS-HDFC, AUTH-PRE, and consistent logging of exceptions.

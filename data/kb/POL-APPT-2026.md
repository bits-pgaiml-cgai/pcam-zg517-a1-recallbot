# Appointment Booking Rules 2026

doc_id: POL-APPT-2026
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Operations > Appointments
codes: SLOT-GEN, SLOT-SPEC, WAIT-24, TELE-OK

Appointment booking rules for ClinicCare in 2026 (current). SLOT-GEN remains 20 minutes. SLOT-SPEC remains 30 minutes. WAIT-24 replaces WAIT-48: new patients should get a SLOT-GEN within 24 hours. TELE-OK marks telehealth-eligible specialties: dermatology, endocrinology, and GP follow-ups. Same-day SLOT-SPEC is allowed without supervisor approval when TELE-OK applies. Patients may hold three future appointments in 2026. Online check-in opens 60 minutes before the slot via the patient portal.  Additional operational guidance for Appointment Booking Rules 2026 emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes SLOT-GEN, SLOT-SPEC, WAIT-24, TELE-OK, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document POL-APPT-2026 version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

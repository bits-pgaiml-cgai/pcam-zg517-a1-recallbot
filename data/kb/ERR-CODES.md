# Portal and billing error codes

doc_id: ERR-CODES
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Support > Errors
codes: E-4010, E-4021, E-5002

Portal error codes. E-4010 means session expired; ask the patient to log in again. E-4021 means insurance eligibility check failed; verify INS-STAR or INS-HDFC card data. E-5002 means payment gateway timeout; retry after five minutes or pay at the counter. Front desk should log each error code with the patient ID in the incident form.  Additional operational guidance for Portal and billing error codes emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes E-4010, E-4021, E-5002, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document ERR-CODES version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

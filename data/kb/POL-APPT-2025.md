# Appointment Booking Rules 2025

doc_id: POL-APPT-2025
version: 2025
valid_from: 2025-01-01
valid_to: 2025-12-31
section: Operations > Appointments
codes: SLOT-GEN, SLOT-SPEC, WAIT-48

Appointment booking rules for ClinicCare in 2025. General physician slots use code SLOT-GEN and are 20 minutes long. Specialist slots use code SLOT-SPEC and are 30 minutes long. WAIT-48 means new patients may wait up to 48 hours for the first available SLOT-GEN. Same-day SLOT-SPEC bookings require supervisor approval in 2025. Patients may hold at most two future appointments across all clinics. Walk-ins are accepted only for SLOT-GEN after 4 pm if capacity remains.  Additional operational guidance for Appointment Booking Rules 2025 emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes SLOT-GEN, SLOT-SPEC, WAIT-48, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document POL-APPT-2025 version 2025 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

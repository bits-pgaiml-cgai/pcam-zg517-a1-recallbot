# Refund and Cancellation Policy 2025

doc_id: POL-REFUND-2025
version: 2025
valid_from: 2025-01-01
valid_to: 2025-12-31
section: Billing > Refunds
codes: P-204, RFD-7D, CXL-24H

ClinicCare refund and cancellation policy for calendar year 2025. Plan code P-204 covers standard outpatient consults. Under P-204 the refund window is 7 calendar days from the payment date when the appointment has not been consumed. Code RFD-7D means a seven-day refund eligibility for unused prepaid consults. Cancellation code CXL-24H requires at least 24 hours notice before the booked slot. Late cancellations within 24 hours forfeit 50 percent of the prepaid fee. No-shows forfeit the full prepaid amount for P-204 visits in 2025. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 10 business days. Emergency department visits and procedure packages are not covered by P-204 refunds. Patients must raise refund requests through the front desk or the patient portal. This 2025 policy is superseded by the 2026 refund policy after 2025-12-31. Keep historical records of 2025 cancellations for audit for twenty-four months.  Additional operational guidance for Refund and Cancellation Policy 2025 emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes P-204, RFD-7D, CXL-24H, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document POL-REFUND-2025 version 2025 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

# Vaccination desk FAQ

doc_id: FAQ-VACCINE
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: FAQ > Vaccines
codes: VAX-FLU, VAX-HEP, VAX-COVID

Vaccination desk FAQ. VAX-FLU is seasonal influenza vaccine offered from June to October. VAX-HEP covers hepatitis B adult schedule. VAX-COVID boosters follow national guidance. Bring prior immunisation card when available. Observation of 15 minutes is mandatory.  Additional operational guidance for Vaccination desk FAQ emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes VAX-FLU, VAX-HEP, VAX-COVID, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document FAQ-VACCINE version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

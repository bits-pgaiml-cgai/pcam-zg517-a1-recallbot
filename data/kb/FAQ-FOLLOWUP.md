# Follow-up visit FAQ

doc_id: FAQ-FOLLOWUP
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: FAQ > Follow-up
codes: FU-7, FU-30

Follow-up FAQ. FU-7 means a free follow-up within 7 days for the same complaint under P-204. FU-30 means a discounted follow-up within 30 days for chronic disease reviews. Telehealth follow-ups are allowed when TELE-OK applies to the specialty.  Additional operational guidance for Follow-up visit FAQ emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes FU-7, FU-30, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document FAQ-FOLLOWUP version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

# In-house pharmacy notes

doc_id: SVC-PHARMACY
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Services > Pharmacy
codes: RX-GEN, RX-CTRL

Pharmacy notes. RX-GEN covers generic substitutions unless the prescription marks dispense as written. RX-CTRL are controlled medicines requiring original prescription and ID verification. Pharmacy at LOC-HYD is open during HRS-WD hours.  Additional operational guidance for In-house pharmacy notes emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes RX-GEN, RX-CTRL, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document SVC-PHARMACY version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

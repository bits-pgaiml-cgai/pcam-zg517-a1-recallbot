# No-show and late policy

doc_id: POL-NOSHOW
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Operations > No-show
codes: NS-1, NS-3

No-show policy. NS-1 is the first no-show warning recorded on the patient profile. NS-3 means three no-shows in six months may require prepaid booking for future SLOT-SPEC. Patients can clear an NS-1 flag by attending the next confirmed appointment on time.  Additional operational guidance for No-show and late policy emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes NS-1, NS-3, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document POL-NOSHOW version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

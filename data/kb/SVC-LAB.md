# Laboratory service notes

doc_id: SVC-LAB
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Services > Lab
codes: LAB-CBC, LAB-LFT, LAB-FAST

Laboratory service notes. Test code LAB-CBC is complete blood count with same-day results before 14:00 if drawn before 10:00. LAB-LFT is liver function panel with 24-hour turnaround. LAB-FAST means 8 to 12 hours fasting is required before the draw. Home collection is available within 8 km of LOC-HYD for an extra fee. Critical values are called to the ordering physician within one hour.  Additional operational guidance for Laboratory service notes emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes LAB-CBC, LAB-LFT, LAB-FAST, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document SVC-LAB version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

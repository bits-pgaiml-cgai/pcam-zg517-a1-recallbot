# Patient privacy and records FAQ

doc_id: FAQ-PRIVACY
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: FAQ > Privacy
codes: REC-REQ, OTP-VIEW

Privacy FAQ. Medical records requests use code REC-REQ and are fulfilled in 5 working days. Portal viewing uses OTP-VIEW one-time passwords sent to the registered mobile number. ClinicCare never shares clinical notes with employers without written consent. Minors need a guardian present for the first visit and for REC-REQ pickup.  Additional operational guidance for Patient privacy and records FAQ emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes REC-REQ, OTP-VIEW, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document FAQ-PRIVACY version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

# Refund and Cancellation Policy 2026

doc_id: POL-REFUND-2026
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Billing > Refunds
codes: P-204, RFD-14D, CXL-12H

ClinicCare refund and cancellation policy for calendar year 2026 (current). Plan code P-204 still labels standard outpatient consults. Under the 2026 rules for P-204 the refund window is 14 calendar days from payment when the appointment is unused. Code RFD-14D replaces RFD-7D and means a fourteen-day refund eligibility. Cancellation code CXL-12H now requires only 12 hours notice before the booked slot. Late cancellations within 12 hours forfeit 40 percent of the prepaid fee. No-shows forfeit 80 percent of the prepaid amount for P-204 visits in 2026. Refunds are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method. Same-day telehealth reschedules are free once per month under plan P-204. This document is the authoritative refund policy while valid_from and valid_to hold. Older 2025 refund rules must not be used for appointments paid in 2026.  Additional operational guidance for Refund and Cancellation Policy 2026 emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes P-204, RFD-14D, CXL-12H, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document POL-REFUND-2026 version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

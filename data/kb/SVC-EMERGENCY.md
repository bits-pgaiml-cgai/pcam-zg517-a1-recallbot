# Urgent care service notes

doc_id: SVC-EMERGENCY
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01
valid_to: 2099-12-31
section: Services > Urgent
codes: URG-TRI, AMB-CALL

Urgent care notes. URG-TRI is nurse triage at LOC-HYD Sunday mornings. AMB-CALL is the ambulance coordination desk number listed on the portal. Chest pain, stroke signs, and major trauma should go to a tertiary ER, not ClinicCare.  Additional operational guidance for Urgent care service notes emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes URG-TRI, AMB-CALL, and consistent logging of exceptions. Staff should confirm patient identity, explain wait times, and cite document SVC-EMERGENCY version 2026 when answering policy questions. Validity window is 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31.

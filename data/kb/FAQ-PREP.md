# Visit preparation FAQ

doc_id: FAQ-PREP
version: 2026
valid_from: 2026-01-01

section: FAQ > Prep

Visit preparation FAQ. PREP-NEW for new patients: arrive 20 minutes early with ID and prior reports. PREP-LAB: follow LAB-FAST instructions when blood work is ordered. Bring a medication list including over-the-counter drugs and supplements.  Additional operational guidance for Visit preparation FAQ emphasises clear communication at the front desk, accurate use of codes PREP-NEW, PREP-LAB, and consistent logging of exceptions.
